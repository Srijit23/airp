# Environmental assets

| Local texture pair | Source | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| meadow, meadow-surface | [Rocky Terrain 02](https://polyhaven.com/a/rocky_terrain_02) | Grassy ground with scattered stones |
| stone, stone-surface | [Aerial Rocks 02](https://polyhaven.com/a/aerial_rocks_02) | Exposed rock faces |
| silt, silt-surface | [Aerial Ground Rock](https://polyhaven.com/a/aerial_ground_rock) | Dry soil and gravel deposits |

The terrain scans are distributed by Poly Haven under [CC0](https://polyhaven.com/license). The preparation script converts the diffuse image to WebP and packs the OpenGL normal X/Y and roughness into a second WebP in RGB order. The original scans are downloaded at preparation time. Runtime files are served from `public/assets/terrain/`.

The elevation field, sky, contour map and interface artwork are generated by AIRP. Three.js, Vite and Playwright retain their upstream package licenses.


## Road surfaces

| Local texture pair | Source | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| asphalt, asphalt-surface | [Worn Asphalt](https://polyhaven.com/a/worn_asphalt) | Carriageways, repairs and off-street parking |
| pavers, pavers-surface | [Floor Pavement](https://polyhaven.com/a/floor_pavement) | Sidewalks, rotary apron and curb detail |

Both Poly Haven scans are [CC0](https://polyhaven.com/license). Run `python scripts/prepare-roads.py` with Pillow installed to rebuild the 1K local WebP maps. The surface maps pack tangent-normal X/Y and roughness into RGB. Street names, directional signs, roadside geometry and markings are generated from the AIRP city plan. Runtime loading uses the copies under `public/assets/roads/`.
