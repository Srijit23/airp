# Environment assets

The homes, city and works libraries contain 21 houses, 19 urban buildings and 20 industrial models from Kenney's [Suburban](https://kenney.nl/assets/city-kit-suburban), [Commercial](https://kenney.nl/assets/city-kit-commercial) and [Industrial](https://kenney.nl/assets/city-kit-industrial) kits. The palm, shrubs, ground plants and rocks come from the [Nature Kit](https://kenney.nl/assets/nature-kit). These assets are CC0. The local glTF meshes are indexed, grounded and rotated to face local +Z. The architectural libraries include simplified distance models.

The canopy geometry and foliage use [EZ Tree](https://github.com/dgreenheck/ez-tree). Its MIT notice is included in public/assets/places/EZ-Tree-LICENSE.txt. The bark image from its bundle is from ambientCG (CC0). The facade colour and normal sheets combine Poly Haven's CC0 concrete_wall_006, red_brick_03, roof_tiles_14 and corrugated_iron_03 scans. World layouts and signs are original to AIRP.
