AIRP Tree Model Showcase
"Oak Tree", "Pine Tree", "Snowy Spruce", "Birch Tree" and "Acacia Tree"
by vinish.dev, https://vinish.dev/free-3d-models/trees
Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

AIRP modifications to all five works: welded and quantized mesh data, a
mesh-simplified second level of detail, Meshopt geometry compression and
embedded WebP textures. Snow and snow mound meshes were removed from the
spruce. Source artwork and original credit metadata remain attributed to the
author. The five originals and their SHA-256 digests are in src/trees/imported.json.
