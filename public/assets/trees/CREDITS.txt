AIRP combines the artist's LOD0 and LOD1 and shares their materials.

AIRP modifications include welded and quantized mesh data,
mesh-simplified distance geometry, Meshopt geometry compression and
embedded WebP textures. Snow and snow mound meshes were removed from the
spruce. Source artwork and original credit metadata remain attributed to the
author. Original filenames and SHA-256 digests are in src/trees/imported.json.
